Case Deep Dive: Trust Account Opening

A bank process that starts with a sales rep and passes through Fiduciary Guidance and up to nine specialist review teams. I built the app, the four automation flows behind it, and the configurable reminder and escalation logic.

  1. Sales rep submits the request

    Enters account details in the Trust Account Opening Power App. Details are stored in the Trust Account Request Form SharePoint list.

  2. Document Risk Review starts

    Initiating the Document Risk Review and Acceptance triggers TAO_Doc Risk Review. It creates a task in SharePoint and emails the task link to Fiduciary Guidance, the Document Risk Review team.

  3. Optional reviews are initiated

    While reviewing, Fiduciary Guidance can start any number of optional reviews out of nine types, such as GST, IRA, ILIT, Closely Held Business, Real Estate, and Estate. This triggers TAO_Optional Reviews, which creates a task and emails the matching team for each review.

  4. Specialists complete their reviews

    Each review is done in a second, dynamic canvas app that adapts to the review type.

  5. Reassign when needed

    If the person who claimed a review can't complete it, they reassign it to a teammate. TAO_Reassign assigns the task to that person and emails them the task link.

  6. Fiduciary Guidance closes the loop

    Once every optional review is complete, Fiduciary Guidance gets an email and can complete the Document Risk Review.

  7. Sales rep is notified

    When the Document Risk Review is approved, the sales rep receives an email confirming approval and the next steps.

Reminders and escalation, controlled by the business

A separate SLA flow sends reminder emails for reviews that aren't complete. The interval (every 2 days by default) and the number of reminders before a review is escalated (3 by default) are configurable, so the team can change them to 5, 6, or any other value without editing a flow.
